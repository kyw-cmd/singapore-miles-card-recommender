Important limitations

Merchant category codes are assigned by acquiring banks and can differ from a merchant's apparent business. Promotions and exclusions change. This prototype therefore exposes the rule assumptions and links to bank sources; confirm high-value transactions against current terms. “Miles” means the airline-mile equivalent of bank points where applicable.
